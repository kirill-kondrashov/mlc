\section{Escape Lemma}

\subsection*{Lemma escape\_lemma}
\textbf{Description:} Escape lemma: if the orbit of z ever leaves the disk of radius R(c), then it
escapes to infinity.

\textbf{Proof Description:} We define a 'growth factor' \texttt{λ(w) = |w| - |c|/|w|}.
We show that if \texttt{|w| > R(c)}, then \texttt{λ(w) > 1}.
We then prove by induction that \texttt{|f\_c\^{}k(w)| $\ge$ |w| * λ(w)\^{}k}.
Since \texttt{λ(w) > 1}, the term \texttt{λ(w)\^{}k} grows to infinity, so the orbit norm grows unbounded.
