\section{Parameter Puzzle Properties}
\label{sec:PuzzleLemmas2}

This section establishes key topological properties of the parameter puzzle pieces $\mathcal{P}_n(c)$, specifically their openness and their role as a neighborhood basis.

\subsection{Openness: \texttt{para\_puzzle\_piece\_open}}

A crucial step in the proof is showing that the parameter puzzle pieces are open sets. This relies on the structural stability of holomorphic motions.

\subsubsection{Holomorphic Motions}
\textbf{Definition}: A \emph{holomorphic motion} of a set $E \subset \mathbb{C}$ over the unit disk $\mathbb{D}$ is a map $f: \mathbb{D} \times E \to \mathbb{C}$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item For fixed $z \in E$, the map $\lambda \mapsto f(\lambda, z)$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$.
    \item For fixed $\lambda \in \mathbb{D}$, the map $z \mapsto f(\lambda, z)$ is injective.
    \item $f(0, z) = z$ for all $z \in E$ (Identity at time 0).
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Slodkowski's Theorem}
We use a powerful result by Z. Slodkowski (Generalized Lambda Lemma):

\textbf{Axiom} (\texttt{slodkowski\_theorem}): Every holomorphic motion of a subset $E \subset \mathbb{C}$ parametrized by the unit disk can be extended to a holomorphic motion of the entire plane $\mathbb{C}$.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Lean]
axiom slodkowski_theorem {E : Set $\mathbb{C}$} (h : HolomorphicMotion E) :
    $\exists$ H : HolomorphicMotion Set.univ,
      ($\forall$ $\lambda$ z, z $\in$ E $\to$ H.val $\lambda$ z = h.val $\lambda$ z)
\end{lstlisting}

\subsubsection{Theorem Application}
The theorem \texttt{para\_puzzle\_piece\_open} states that each parameter puzzle piece $\mathcal{P}_n$ is an open subset of the complex plane.

\textit{Proof Sketch.}
The proof relies on the stability of the dynamical system under small perturbations of the parameter $c$.
1.  We construct a holomorphic motion of the boundary of the dynamical puzzle piece $P_n(c)$ as $c$ varies in a small disk. This follows from the fact that the boundary consists of equipotentials and external rays which move holomorphically (Axiom \texttt{puzzle\_boundary\_motion\_exists}).
2.  By Slodkowski's Theorem, this motion extends to a motion of the entire plane.
3.  The extended motion provides a conjugation between the dynamics at $c_0$ and nearby $c$.
4.  The property defining the puzzle piece (orbit of 0 relative to the puzzle boundary) is preserved by this conjugation.
5.  Thus, if $c_0 \in \mathcal{P}_n$, then all sufficiently close $c$ are also in $\mathcal{P}_n$. This implies openness. \qed

\subsection{Connectivity: \texttt{para\_puzzle\_piece\_inter\_mandelbrot\_connected}}

We also prove that the intersection of a parameter puzzle piece with the Mandelbrot set is connected.
\[ \text{IsConnected}(\mathcal{P}_n \cap \mathcal{M}) \]
This actually simplifies significantly because $\mathcal{M} \subseteq \mathcal{P}_n$ for all $n$ (by construction of the puzzle pieces based on Green's function, which is 0 on $\mathcal{M}$). Thus, the intersection is just $\mathcal{M}$ itself, which is connected (Douady-Hubbard).

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Lean]
theorem para_puzzle_piece_inter_mandelbrot_connected (n : $\mathbb{N}$) :
    IsConnected (ParaPuzzlePiece n $\cap$ MandelbrotSet)
\end{lstlisting}
