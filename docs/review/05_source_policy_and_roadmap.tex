\chapter{Source policy and roadmap}

\section{ArXiv sources}

For every arXiv reference used in a formalization task, the repository keeps:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the arXiv identifier and version metadata;
\item the compressed source archive;
\item an extracted source tree; and
\item the selected main \TeX{} file.
\end{enumerate}
The executable policy is implemented by
\lean{scripts/fetch_arxiv_sources.py} and the manifest
\lean{refs/mlc_sources.json}.  This is preferable to copying a single PDF:
definitions, theorem labels, and notation can be searched directly while
formalizing.

The current manifest includes the 2025--2026 additions
\lean{2509.23031}, \lean{2606.27272}, \lean{2609.15500},
\lean{2609.37383}, and \lean{2610.25983}.  The review records their
mathematical role separately from the older MLC-case papers: some are direct
local-connectivity theorems, while others provide neutral-renormalization,
rigidity, or hybrid-class machinery.
The preprint \lean{2606.27272} also cites \emph{Elephant puzzles}
as in preparation for a puzzle Teichmüller-diameter input.
An unpublished dependency is recorded as such; no downloadable
source archive or proof is invented for it.

\section{Books and research-paper series}

Lyubich's author page lists the online \emph{Conformal Geometry and
Dynamics} book as volumes I--II.  The local 2013 ICTP notes are a
separate manuscript version.  \emph{Dynamics of quadratic
polynomials, III} and \emph{A priori bounds, IV: Elephant Eyes}
belong to research-paper series, not additional volumes of that book.
The source inventory preserves these distinctions.  The preface of volumes I--II (printed p.~5) plans
Volumes~III (the complete picture in the real quadratic family) and~IV (advances in the MLC).  Neither is
published, so neither is cited.

\section{Removal of duplicates}

The reference directory is not cleaned by filename heuristics.  A duplicate
is removed only after matching its identifier, version, checksum, and
provenance.  Research notes remain separate from primary sources because
they document failed proof attempts and rejected reductions.

\section{Work packages}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Normalize the source inventory and verify all arXiv versions.
\item Replace the audited Gaussian-area proxy with genuine conformal
      modulus and retain the obstruction theorems as regression guards.
\item Finish the common analytic foundations: complex derivatives,
      polynomial-like maps, Green functions, Böttcher coordinates, and
      modulus.
\item Replace informal puzzle objects with explicit graph-cut dynamical and
      parameter puzzles.
\item Formalize the phase--parameter map and its connectedness consequences.
\item Assemble the finitely renormalizable/Yoccoz theorem.
\item Formalize the principal nest and a priori-bound interfaces used by
      Lyubich.
\item Add the bounded-type satellite and Feigenbaum combinatorial predicates
      and prove the corresponding local-connectivity implications.
\item Revisit the global root only after the parameter-class theorems are
      available; retain every genuinely open existence statement as an
      explicit proposition.
\end{enumerate}

The present book increment adds mathematical foundations,
compact-closure endpoints, and a checked model audit.  It remains
short of complete literature coverage and of the planned full-length
treatment.  Some frozen public interfaces require replacement,
rather than an existence proof: the audit has established that their
inputs are impossible.
