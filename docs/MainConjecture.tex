\section{Main Conjecture Statement}
\label{sec:MainConjecture}

The central goal of this formalization is the statement of the MLC conjecture for quadratic polynomials.

\subsection{Conjecture: \texttt{main\_conjecture}}

\textbf{Definition}: The \emph{Mandelbrot set} $\mathcal{M}$ is the set of parameters $c \in \mathbb{C}$ for which the orbit of 0 under $f_c(z) = z^2 + c$ remains bounded.
\[ \mathcal{M} = \{ c \in \mathbb{C} \mid \exists B, \forall n, |f_c^n(0)| \le B \} \]

The conjecture states that $\mathcal{M}$ is locally connected.

\textbf{Theorem} (\texttt{main\_conjecture}): The Mandelbrot set is locally connected.

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Lean]
theorem main_conjecture : IsLocallyConnected MandelbrotSet
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Reduction to Yoccoz's Theorem}

The proof strategy formalized here follows the reduction to Yoccoz's Theorem.
It is known that local connectivity is equivalent to the shrinking of puzzle pieces for non-renormalizable parameters (and related conditions for renormalizable ones).

The formal proof combines:
1.  \texttt{yoccoz\_theorem}: Shrinking of puzzle pieces implies local connectivity at $c$ for non-renormalizable parameters.
2.  \texttt{mlc\_infinitely\_renorm}: MLC holds at infinitely renormalizable parameters (assumed as an axiom/deep theorem).
3.  \texttt{finitely\_renorm\_reduces\_to\_yoccoz}: The finitely renormalizable case reduces to the non-renormalizable case via renormalization.
