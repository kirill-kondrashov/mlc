# Sequence of work

12–15 hours per week. [Statement and continuation criterion](research_passport.md).
Stages move back until their prerequisite proofs are complete.

| Weeks | Written output | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Exact admissibility condition, genuine returns, source comparison | Select a substantive new class |
| 3–4 | Analytic lemma on an unbounded family | Check losses and the separation mechanism |
| 5–6 | Complete lemma and existence of the required returns | Obtain M1 realization |
| 7–8 | A new parameter outside the published class and its MLC | Establish an extension of the range |
| 9–10 | Article draft and revisions after discussion | Check completeness of the argument |
| 11–12 | Final checks, preprint, and submission decision | Finish once the proof is complete |

6 hours on proofs, 2 on sources, 2 on writing, 1 on checks,
1 on the log; devote the optional three hours to the current missing lemma.
[Log](progress.csv). Global `Coverage` is a separate task.
