## Established reduction

The image of the free group's first stabilizer consists of pairs with equal
exponent sums of $b$. This gives a fiber product for abelian portraits and
$r_m=2r_{m-1}-1$, proving B1-R completely. Every step is in the audit.

The exact published location of the formula for our portraits has not been
found. This does not prevent retiring it as the primary candidate:
an independent substantive contribution has not been justified.

## Completed B2-N reduction

Theorem 4.7(i) supplies independent integral commutators at every leaf.
Corollary 4.11 and the marked-generator check identify the free and faithful
class-2 quotients. Abelian constraints follow from the first stabilizer;
an explicit homomorphism recovers the root. The full image, center,
and abelian quotient are computed in the [audit](B2_novelty_audit.md).
No additional uncovered step remains in the stated problem.
