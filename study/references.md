# Sources for the steps of M1

[Statement](research/MLC_bridge.md), [root status](../README.md).

1. **Returns and puzzles:** [Lyubich I–II](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~mlyubich/book.pdf),
   local version `refs/Lyubich_book_vol_I-II_2023.pdf`.
   Define genuine domains and their `ReturnBudget` quantities.
   Read proofs of the lemmas used. Selective copying of statements can
   slow familiarization with the form of the text; produce a dependency map.
2. **Analytic estimate:** [Kahn–Lyubich, Quasi-Additivity](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v169-n2-p05.pdf),
   p. 564, §3.1.3, Appendix §4. Check collars, local and total degrees,
   the threshold, and existing bounded-degree blocks.
3. **Phase–parameter transfer:** [Lyubich III, Parapuzzle](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9606219).
   Compare its return class and transfer constants with the proposed M1.
4. **Limits and novelty:** [Dudko, §4.4–4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24171v1),
   [Blokh–Levin–Oversteegen–Timorin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03157),
   [Kahn–Kapiamba–Lyubich](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27272).
   Separately define the published class to be extended.
   The listed open problems are not assumed solved.
5. **Biset model:** [Bartholdi–Dudko I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.08539),
   [II](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04059),
   [Bartholdi–Dudko–Pilgrim](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.15548v2).
   Relate sections and degrees to the chosen geometric returns.
   For weighted graphs, compare with
   [D. Thurston](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2020/192-1/p01).

Use [Mikhailov–Passi](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-85818-8)
for a specific question about group quotients.
