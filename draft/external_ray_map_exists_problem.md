# Remaining root problem at c = 2

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let

$$
U_\infty=\{z\in\mathbb C : f^{\circ n}(z)\to\infty \text{ as } n\to\infty\},
\qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C : |w|>1\},
\qquad
V=\{z\in\mathbb C : |z|>4\},
$$

and let

$$
\phi:U_\infty\to\Omega
$$

be the normalized Böttcher coordinate of $$f$$, characterized by

$$
\phi(f(z))=\phi(z)^2 \quad \text{for } z\in U_\infty,
\qquad
\frac{\phi(z)}{z}\to 1 \quad \text{as } z\to\infty.
$$

## Problem 1. Basin-valued exterior inverse

Prove that there exists a map

$$
\Psi:\Omega\to U_\infty
$$

such that

$$
\phi(\Psi(w))=w \quad \text{for every } w\in\Omega,
$$

and

$$
\Psi(\phi(z))=z \quad \text{for every } z\in V.
$$
