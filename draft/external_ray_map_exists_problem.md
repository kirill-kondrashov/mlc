# Remaining exact problem statements at $c = 2$

Let

$$
V=\{z\in\mathbb C:\lvert z\rvert>4\}, \qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C:\lvert w\rvert>1\},
$$

and let

$$
\phi:V\to\Omega
$$

be the restricted outside Böttcher map for the quadratic polynomial

$$
f(z)=z^2+2.
$$

The remaining work is exactly the following two statements.

## Problem A. Covering-degree rigidity

Assume that:

1. $\phi$ is proper;
2. $\phi$ is a local homeomorphism;
3. there exists an integer $d\ge 1$ such that every fiber $\phi^{-1}(w)$, $w\in\Omega$, has cardinality exactly $d$;
4. for some $R>4$, the loop
   $$
   \gamma_R:S^1\to\Omega,\qquad \gamma_R(e^{2\pi i t})=\phi(Re^{2\pi i t}),
   $$
   is freely homotopic in $\Omega$ to the positively oriented standard circle
   $$
   \sigma_R:S^1\to\Omega,\qquad \sigma_R(e^{2\pi i t})=Re^{2\pi i t}.
   $$

Prove that $d=1$.

Equivalently: prove that $\phi$ is a degree-one covering of $\Omega$, hence every fiber of $\phi$ consists of exactly one point.

## Problem B. Properness and local homeomorphy

Prove that the same map $\phi:V\to\Omega$ is:

1. proper;
2. a local homeomorphism.
