# Exterior Böttcher inverse at \(c=2\)

Let
\[
f_2(z)=z^2+2,
\qquad
\phi_2:\mathcal B_\infty(2)\to\{w\in\mathbb C:|w|>1\}
\]
be the Böttcher coordinate of \(f_2\), normalized by
\[
\phi_2(f_2(z))=\phi_2(z)^2,
\qquad
\phi_2(z)\sim z \quad (z\to\infty).
\]

Prove that there exists a map
\[
\Psi_2:\{w\in\mathbb C:|w|>1\}\to\mathbb C
\]
such that
\[
\phi_2(\Psi_2(w))=w
\quad\forall\,w\in\mathbb C,\ |w|>1,
\]
and
\[
\Psi_2(\phi_2(z))=z
\quad\forall\,z\in\mathbb C,\ |z|>4.
\]
