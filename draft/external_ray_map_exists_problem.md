# Remaining \(c=2\) exterior Böttcher seam

Let
\[
f_2(z)=z^2+2,
\qquad
\phi_2:\mathcal B_\infty(2)\to\Omega:=\{w\in\mathbb C:|w|>1\}
\]
be the normalized Böttcher coordinate, so that
\[
\phi_2(f_2(z))=\phi_2(z)^2,
\qquad
\phi_2(z)\sim z \quad (z\to\infty).
\]

Prove the following two statements.

\[
(\mathrm{R})\quad
\exists\,R_2:\Omega\to\mathbb C
\ \text{such that}\ 
\phi_2(R_2(w))=w
\quad\forall\,w\in\Omega.
\]

\[
(\mathrm{I})\quad
\forall\,z_1,z_2\in\mathbb C,\ 
\bigl(|z_1|>4 \wedge |z_2|>4 \wedge \phi_2(z_1)=\phi_2(z_2)\bigr)
\ \Longrightarrow\ 
z_1=z_2.
\]

Then the desired external-ray package follows formally: there exists
\[
\Psi_2:\Omega\to\mathbb C
\]
such that
\[
\phi_2(\Psi_2(w))=w
\quad\forall\,w\in\Omega,
\]
and
\[
\Psi_2(\phi_2(z))=z
\quad\forall\,z\in\mathbb C,\ |z|>4.
\]

Equivalently: the only remaining mathematical input is to establish exterior
surjectivity of \(\phi_2\) together with injectivity of \(\phi_2\) on the
canonical outside region \(\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>4\}\).
