# Remaining problem 2: basin-valued inverse for the genuine coordinate at c = 2

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let

$$
U_\infty=\{z\in\mathbb C : f^{\circ n}(z)\to\infty \text{ as } n\to\infty\},
\qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C : |w|>1\},
\qquad
V=\{z\in\mathbb C : |z|>4\},
$$

and let

$$
\Phi:U_\infty\to\Omega
$$

be a map satisfying the conclusions of Problem 1.

## Problem 2. Basin-valued inverse package

Prove that there exists a map

$$
\Psi:\Omega\to U_\infty
$$

such that

$$
\Phi(\Psi(w))=w
\qquad \text{for every } w\in\Omega,
$$

and

$$
\Psi(\Phi(z))=z
\qquad \text{for every } z\in V.
$$

Equivalently, prove that $$\Phi$$ is a conformal isomorphism from $$U_\infty$$ onto $$\Omega$$ and that its inverse $$\Psi=\Phi^{-1}$$ satisfies

$$
\Psi(\Phi(z))=z
\qquad \text{for every } z\in V.
$$
