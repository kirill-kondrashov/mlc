# Failure of the fixed-anchor Green-ray inequality

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let $$G:\mathbb C\to\mathbb R$$ be the Green function of $$f$$, and for each nonzero $$w\in\mathbb C$$ define the anchored point on the Euclidean ray of $$w$$ by

$$
a(w)=4\,\frac{w}{|w|}.
$$

## Obstruction 2. The global fixed-anchor inequality is false

Show that the statement

$$
G(a(w))<\log |w|
\qquad \text{for every } w\in\mathbb C \text{ with } |w|>1
$$

is false.
