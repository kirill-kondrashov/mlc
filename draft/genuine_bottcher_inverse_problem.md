# Remaining problem 2: exterior inverse package for the genuine Böttcher coordinate at $$c=2$$

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let

$$
K(f)=\{z\in\mathbb C : \{f^{\circ n}(z)\}_{n\geq 0} \text{ is bounded}\},
\qquad
U_\infty=\mathbb C\setminus K(f),
\qquad
V=\{z\in\mathbb C : |z|>4\},
\qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C : |w|>1\},
$$

and let

$$
\Phi:U_\infty\to\Omega
$$

be a map satisfying the conclusions of Problem 1.

Prove that there exists a map

$$
\Psi:\Omega\to U_\infty
$$

such that

$$
\Phi(\Psi(w))=w
\qquad \text{for every } w\in\Omega,
$$

and

$$
\Psi(\Phi(z))=z
\qquad \text{for every } z\in V.
$$
