# Remaining sufficient injectivity problem at $c=2$

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let

$$
U_\infty=\{z\in\mathbb C : f^{\circ n}(z)\to\infty \text{ as } n\to\infty\},
\qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C : |w|>1\},
\qquad
V=\{z\in\mathbb C : |z|>4\},
$$

and let

$$
\phi:U_\infty\to\Omega
$$

be the normalized Böttcher coordinate of $$f$$, characterized by

$$
\phi(f(z))=\phi(z)^2 \quad \text{for } z\in U_\infty,
\qquad
\frac{\phi(z)}{z}\to 1 \quad \text{as } z\to\infty.
$$

## Problem 2. Injectivity on the outside-open region

Prove that the restriction

$$
\phi|_V:V\to\Omega
$$

is injective. Equivalently, prove that whenever

$$
z_1,z_2\in V
\qquad \text{and} \qquad
\phi(z_1)=\phi(z_2),
$$

then

$$
z_1=z_2.
$$
