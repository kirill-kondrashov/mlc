# Remaining Green-ray anchor-gap problem at c = 2

Let

$$
f(z)=z^2+2,
$$

let

$$
U_\infty=\{z\in\mathbb C : f^{\circ n}(z)\to\infty \text{ as } n\to\infty\},
\qquad
\Omega=\{w\in\mathbb C : |w|>1\},
$$

and let

$$
\phi:U_\infty\to\Omega
$$

be the normalized Böttcher coordinate of $$f$$, characterized by

$$
\phi(f(z))=\phi(z)^2 \quad \text{for } z\in U_\infty,
\qquad
\frac{\phi(z)}{z}\to 1 \quad \text{as } z\to\infty.
$$

Define the Green function of $$f$$ on $$U_\infty$$ by

$$
G(z)=\log |\phi(z)|.
$$

For each $$w\in\Omega$$, define the anchored point on the ray of $$w$$ by

$$
a(w)=4\,\frac{w}{|w|}.
$$

## Problem 2. Green-ray anchor gap

Prove that for every $$w\in\Omega$$,

$$
G(a(w))<\log |w|.
$$

Equivalently, prove that

$$
G\!\left(4\,\frac{w}{|w|}\right)<\log |w|
\qquad \text{for every } w\in\mathbb C \text{ with } |w|>1.
$$
