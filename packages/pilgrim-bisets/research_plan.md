# Continuing B3

8 November 2026. Work is deferred until time is allocated separately.
The package goal is the full B3 theorem in the context of Pilgrim's conjecture.

1. Derive the biset recursion from the Shepelevtseva–Timorin mating tree;
   match labels, peripheral classes, and the known equator.
   This fixes the initial curve-pullback model.
2. Implement normal coordinates of simple curves and their pullbacks.
   Check closure of a trial attractor list.
3. Compute pullbacks of a family with arbitrary twist exponent.
   The audit excludes partition-only heights and strong virtual-endomorphism
   contraction: `ρ(φ_F) ≥ 1`.
4. Construct a height incorporating twists, or another complete global
   attraction argument. Prove the result for every curve.
5. Update the literature comparison and assemble the paper after proving it.

A general pullback lemma may enter the main MLC programme once it has
analytic estimates and an application to genuine quadratic returns.
