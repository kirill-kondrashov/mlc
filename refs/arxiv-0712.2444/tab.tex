

\subsection{Table of notations}

$p$ is the renormalization period of $f$;

$\gamma$ is a dividing periodic point of period $\bt$, 

$\bgamma$ is its cycle;

$\s$ is the numner of rays landing at $\gamma$;   

$\alpha$ is a dividing periodic point of periods $\r=\bt\s$;

$\alpha'=-\alpha$, $\alpha_j=f^j\alpha$;

$\balpha=\{\alpha_j\}_{j=0}^{p-1}$ is the cycle of $\alpha$; 

$\q$ is the number of rays landing at $\alpha$;

$\n$ is the first moment such that $f^{\r\q\n}(0)$ is separated from $0$ by the rays 
      landing at $\alpha'$,

 $\k= \r\q\n$;

$\bl= \k+1+\r^2$ is such a depth that the stars $S^\bl(\alpha_j)$, $j=0,1,\dots, \r-1$,
       are all disjoint;
