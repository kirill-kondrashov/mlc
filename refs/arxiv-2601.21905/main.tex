\title[Elephant Eyes]{A priori bounds for some\\ infinitely renormalizable quadratics:\\
  {\tiny IV. Elephant Eyes } }

\author {Jeremy Kahn and Misha Lyubich}

\begin{abstract} 
  In this paper we prove {\it a priori} bounds for  an ``elephant eye'' combinatorics.
  Little $M$-copies specifying these
  combinatorics are allowed to converge to the cusp of the Mandelbrot set.
  To handle it, we develope a new geometric tool:
  uniform thin-thick decompositions for bordered Riemann surfaces.   
\end{abstract}
